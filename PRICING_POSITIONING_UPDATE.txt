POSITIONING STRATEGY
================================================================================

Core Message:
  "Automatiza tus ventas en WhatsApp — Para cualquier negocio"
  (Automate your sales on WhatsApp — For any business)

Primary ICP: Travel agencies (strongest use case, existing market presence)
Secondary ICPs: Real estate, clinics, ecommerce, coaching, education, services

Pricing Positioning:
  • Not by industry, but by business scale and capability needs
  • Same three tiers for all verticals
  • Features remain generic (conversations, panel, reporting, integrations, SLA)
  • Onboarding templates customized per industry (as optional add-on)


MESSAGING FRAMEWORK
================================================================================

Básico ("Negocios en inicio")
├─ Target: Founders testing product-market fit
├─ Use case: 1-2 person team, validating WhatsApp sales channel
├─ Limit: 200 conversations/month (budget constraint, not capability)
└─ Positioning: "Test drive Mia risk-free"

Pro ("Negocios en crecimiento")
├─ Target: Scale-ups and SMBs with established sales process
├─ Use case: Team of 3+ people relying on WhatsApp revenue
├─ Feature: Unlimited conversations (signal: you've unlocked the channel)
└─ Positioning: "Your sales team on steroids"

Enterprise ("Empresas grandes y cadenas")
├─ Target: Multi-location companies and chains
├─ Use case: Complex integrations, compliance, SLA requirements
├─ Feature: Multi-number, API, dedicated support, 99.9% SLA
└─ Positioning: "Infrastructure-grade reliability"

COMPETITIVE ADVANTAGE
================================================================================

Before: "We help hotels sell more on WhatsApp"
  • Narrow TAM
  • Competing on hospitality features (PMS integration, etc.)
  • Vulnerable to vertical-specific competitors

After: "We automate sales on WhatsApp for any business"
  • 5-10x bigger TAM (all SMBs using WhatsApp for sales)
  • Competing on platform capability (conversations, AI, integrations)
  • Travel becomes a beachhead, not the whole market
  • Blue ocean vs. Respond.io, Zendesk, etc. (they focus on support, we focus on sales)


PRICE-TO-VALUE ALIGNMENT
================================================================================

Pricing now aligns with business growth stage, not industry:

Básico (S/399):
✓ No PMS integration needed (early stage businesses don't have complex systems)
✓ 200 conversations enough to validate product-market fit
✓ Low risk for buyer (easy to switch if it doesn't work)

Pro (S/699):
✓ Unlimited conversations (company has validated WhatsApp channel)
✓ Dashboard, reporting, integrations (operational scale requirements)
✓ Priority support (relying on this for revenue)

Enterprise (S/1,199 + S/500 setup):
✓ Multi-number (managing multiple channels or locations)
✓ API access (building custom workflows)
✓ Dedicated AM and SLA (mission-critical to operations)
