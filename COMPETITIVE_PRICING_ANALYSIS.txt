PRICING STRATEGY BY SEGMENT
================================================================================

FOR SMALL HOTELS (1-2 person operation)
────────────────────────────────────────
Respond.io Starter:     $79/month
Mia Básico:             $108/month (~37% more)
Winner: Respond.io (cheaper + 5x more conversations)

But Mia's advantage: Better AI for sales (not customer support)
Use case: If hotel wants sales automation → Mia. If wants inbox → Respond.io

FOR MID-SIZE AGENCIES (3-5 person team)
────────────────────────────────────────
Respond.io Growth:      $159 + $48 (extra 5 users @ $12c) = $207/month
Intercom Advanced:      $85 × 3 seats = $255/month
Mia Pro:                $189/month ← CLEAR WINNER

Mia advantage: $18-66/month cheaper AND includes AI sales automation

FOR LARGE CHAINS (10+ locations, 20+ people)
────────────────────────────────────────────
Respond.io Advanced:    $279 + $120 (extra 10 users @ $12c) = $399/month
Intercom Expert:        $132 × 20 seats = $2,640/month
Mia Enterprise:         $1,199 + $500 = $1,699/month

Problem: Mia doesn't have team seat pricing
Solution: Add optional team seat add-ons


COMPETITIVE POSITIONING
================================================================================

RESPOND.IO Strengths:
✅ Cheapest entry point ($79)
✅ Multi-channel (SMS, email, calls, Instagram, FB)
✅ More conversation limits at lower price points
✅ Explicit user seat pricing (transparent)
❌ Not designed for sales (their docs emphasize "inbox culture")
❌ Weaker AI (basic AI agents, not sales-specific)
❌ Focuses on marketing/support, not sales funnel

INTERCOM Strengths:
✅ Best-in-class support AI (Fin Agent)
✅ True omnichannel (supports media-rich conversations)
✅ 24/7 multilingual support
✅ Built-in Help Center, knowledge base, etc.
❌ Most expensive (seat-based × team size = $$$)
❌ Over-engineered for small teams
❌ Not designed for sales (support tool, not sales)
❌ Requires significant implementation effort

MIA Strengths:
✅ Purpose-built for sales (Challenger Sale framework, SPIN selling, etc.)
✅ Cheapest per conversation when unlimited (S/699 vs $159 respond.io)
✅ Autonomous 24/7 (no human agents needed at lower tiers)
✅ Fully autonomous AI (doesn't require human team inbox monitoring)
✅ Industry-agnostic (works for any small business, not just hotels)
✅ Fastest time-to-value (no team training needed)
❌ Single-channel (WhatsApp only)
❌ No team seat pricing (can't upsell to larger teams)
❌ Limited integrations (vs. competitors)
❌ No multi-user collaboration features

MIA Weaknesses (vs. Respond.io):
❌ Fewer conversation limits at entry price point
❌ No multi-channel (Respond.io does SMS, FB, Insta, etc.)
❌ Less transparent pricing break-down
❌ Lacks explicit user seat tiers

MIA Weaknesses (vs. Intercom):
❌ Weaker ecosystem (no Help Center, knowledge base)
❌ No true omnichannel
❌ Can't handle customer support (only sales)
❌ No SLA guarantees
